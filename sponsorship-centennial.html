Two organizations, two purposes. The Centennial Mural, the SOHA Shine LA Service Day & Tree Day, and the student art contest and Centennial Pop-Up Museum are programs of the Sherman Oaks Chamber Foundation, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit that fosters community improvement through targeted investments in public art, beautification, arts events and other enhancements to the physical aesthetic of our neighborhood (EIN 46-3144630). Contributions may be tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law, less the fair market value of any goods or services received. The Sherman Oaks Street Fair is produced by the Sherman Oaks/Encino Chamber of Commerce; sponsorship is generally a business expense rather than a charitable contribution. Series packages are invoiced accordingly. Please consult your tax advisor.

Packages, prices and benefits are summarized from the committee’s sponsorship materials. Attendance and audience figures are the organizers’ estimates. A sponsorship is confirmed only in writing; see our Terms of Use.
