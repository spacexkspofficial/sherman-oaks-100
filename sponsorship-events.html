The Centennial Mural

Unveiling Saturday, January 23, 2027

An original work of art on Ventura Blvd., painted by a local artist, with a celebration following the unveiling. Sponsors are named on a permanent bronze donor plaque at the site, and their logo appears on the SOHA Shine LA T-shirt.

  • PresentingTop plaque position · podium recognition at the unveiling · 10 invitations$5,000
  • MuralistLarge name, second-tier plaque position · 6 invitations$2,500
  • PaletteThird-tier plaque position · 4 invitations$1,500
  • BrushstrokeName on fourth-tier plaque · 2 invitations$1,000

The mural is an original work of art and carries no commercial content. Sponsor recognition appears on the adjacent bronze donor plaque.

SOHA Shine LA Service Day & Tree Day

Saturday, April 24, 2027

100 Trees + 100 Yrs Project · 100 trees for 100 years.

Run with the Mayor’s Office Shine LA program, the SOHA Beautification Committee and Volunteers Cleaning Communities. Litter removal, tree-well restoration and 100 new or restored street trees.

  • Block SponsorAdopt a block · sign on that block · name on the T-shirt$500
  • Single TreeName on one tree tag · name on the T-shirt$350

In-kind help is welcome: dumpsters and hauling, gloves and grabbers, coffee, water, tools and trees.

100 Years / Young Artists

August 18 through Labor Day, 2027

Past and Future: the Centennial Pop-Up Historical Museum, and a student art contest where young artists imagine our future.

  • MuseumNaming rights at the pop-up museum space · entrance and exhibit signage$2,500
  • Student Art DisplayNaming rights at Student Art Display or Centennial pop-up museum$2,500
  • ExhibitorSignage at the Centennial pop-up museum or Student Art Display$500

Both $2,500 levels include naming rights, and the flyer lists the pop-up museum under each. Which space your naming covers, and whether it is exclusive, is confirmed with availability before you commit.

35th Sherman Oaks Street Fair

Sunday, October 17, 2027 · Centennial Edition

The largest single-day event in the San Fernando Valley. Organizers estimate 50,000–100,000 attendees, 2+ hours average time on site and $120K+ average household income.

  • TitleEvent naming, main stage signage, 20×20 booth, 20 VIP invitations, 10 parking passes$25,000
  • PlatinumCarnival entrance signage, 10×30 booth, 14 VIP invitations, 6 parking passes$15,000
  • GoldZone naming, 10×30 booth, 10 VIP invitations, 6 parking passes$10,000
  • SilverAttraction naming, 10×20 booth, 6 VIP invitations, 4 parking passes$5,000
  • SharedFull logo package and emcee recognition, no booth required$3,500
  • Bronze10×10 booth in the Sponsor Zone, 2 VIP invitations, 1 parking pass$3,100

All tiers include a logo on the main stage banner, LED screen, website, social media and newsletter, plus emcee recognition on both stages. First come, first served.

Two organizations, two purposes. The Centennial Mural, the SOHA Shine LA Service Day & Tree Day, and the student art contest and Centennial Pop-Up Museum are programs of the Sherman Oaks Chamber Foundation, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit that fosters community improvement through targeted investments in public art, beautification, arts events and other enhancements to the physical aesthetic of our neighborhood (EIN 46-3144630). Contributions may be tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law, less the fair market value of any goods or services received. The Sherman Oaks Street Fair is produced by the Sherman Oaks/Encino Chamber of Commerce; sponsorship is generally a business expense rather than a charitable contribution. Series packages are invoiced accordingly. Please consult your tax advisor.

Packages, prices and benefits are summarized from the committee’s sponsorship materials. Attendance and audience figures are the organizers’ estimates. A sponsorship is confirmed only in writing; see our Terms of Use.
