Full package benefits

One commitment · all four events

Every Series package includes a place in all four celebrations and recognition on the permanent mural donor plaque. Compare Street Fair benefits, recognition at the other events and package limits below.

Centennial Founder

Limit one

By inquiry

The entire centennial branded “Sherman Oaks 100, presented by [Sponsor].”

  • Street Fair: Title Sponsor with event naming, main stage signage, a 20×20 booth, 20 backstage VIP invitations and 10 VIP parking passes.
  • Other three events: Presenting Sponsor of the mural, Shine LA day and pop-up museum.
  • Lasting recognition: Top position on the permanent mural donor plaque and your name in the centennial time capsule.
  • Exclusivity: Category exclusivity for the Centennial Founder.
Email about Centennial Founder

Centennial Legacy

Limit three

$25,000

  • Street Fair: Platinum sponsorship with carnival entrance signage, a 10×30 booth, 14 backstage VIP invitations and 6 VIP parking passes.
  • Other three events: Presenting Sponsor of your choice of the mural, Shine LA day or pop-up museum, plus featured recognition at the other two.
  • Lasting recognition: Your name in the first position on the permanent mural donor plaque.

Value if purchased separately: $27,500

This package sponsors the 2027 events. It is separate from the committee’s Centennial Legacy projects for trees, small businesses and scholarships.

Email about Centennial Legacy

Centennial Champion

Limit six

$15,000

  • Street Fair: Gold sponsorship with zone naming, a 10×30 booth and 10 backstage VIP invitations. Zones include the Community Stage, Pet Zone, Ferris Wheel, Wellness Zone and Youth Entrepreneurs Zone.
  • Other three events: Second-tier sponsorship at the mural, Shine LA day and pop-up museum.
  • Lasting recognition: Your name in the second position on the permanent mural donor plaque.

Value if purchased separately: $17,500

Two organizations, two purposes. The Centennial Mural, the SOHA Shine LA Service Day & Tree Day, and the student art contest and Centennial Pop-Up Museum are programs of the Sherman Oaks Chamber Foundation, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit that fosters community improvement through targeted investments in public art, beautification, arts events and other enhancements to the physical aesthetic of our neighborhood (EIN 46-3144630). Contributions may be tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law, less the fair market value of any goods or services received. The Sherman Oaks Street Fair is produced by the Sherman Oaks/Encino Chamber of Commerce; sponsorship is generally a business expense rather than a charitable contribution. Series packages are invoiced accordingly. Please consult your tax advisor.

Packages, prices and benefits are summarized from the committee’s sponsorship materials. Attendance and audience figures are the organizers’ estimates. A sponsorship is confirmed only in writing; see our Terms of Use.
