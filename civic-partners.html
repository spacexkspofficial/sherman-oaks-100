Community Partnerships

Our civic partners.

The organizations that already do the work of community life in Sherman Oaks — and who help shape the centennial year alongside the committee.

Neighborhood Voice

Neighborhood Council

The Sherman Oaks Neighborhood Council and adjacent neighborhood councils — local representation and resident voice.

Specific partners listed once formal letters of partnership are in place.

Business Community

Chamber & BID

The Sherman Oaks chamber community and the Village at Sherman Oaks Business Improvement District — anchoring the business and boulevard side of the centennial.

Final list of business partners to be confirmed during planning.

Schools

Local schools

Public and private schools in Sherman Oaks join through curriculum, art contests, history projects, and youth-led centennial work.

Specific school partners to be confirmed once principals and PTA leads sign on.

Houses of Worship

Faith communities

Long-standing houses of worship in the neighborhood — anchoring the multi-faith civic-life thread of the centennial.

Participating congregations to be listed once partnerships are confirmed.

Public Institutions

Libraries & parks

Local public libraries and the parks system — venues for events, exhibits, oral histories, and youth programming.

Civic Groups

Service clubs & volunteer orgs

Rotary, Lions, Kiwanis, mutual-aid groups, and other long-running volunteer organizations active in Sherman Oaks.

Arts & Culture

Cultural organizations

Local arts organizations, performance venues, and cultural groups partnering on programming, public art, and exhibits.

History

Historical & archive partners

Los Angeles Public Library, regional archives, the LA Conservancy, and family/community historians anchoring the centennial archive.
