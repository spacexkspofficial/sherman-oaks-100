How it runs

A working committee, not a board.

The steering committee meets monthly during the run-up to the centennial year and biweekly during 2027. Working groups meet on their own cadence. Meetings are open to neighbors who'd like to attend — write to hello@example.com for the next meeting's time and place.

Want to join?

We're always glad to add neighbors who can commit a little ongoing time to one of the working groups. Get in touch through the volunteer form and note which working group sounds like a fit.
