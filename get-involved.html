Volunteer · Partner · Contribute

Help build a year of community.

The Sherman Oaks Centennial is genuinely community-built. Below are the ways people are stepping in — pick whichever fits your time, skills, or interests.

Time

Volunteer at events

A few hours at a centennial event — greeting, setup, info table, kids' activity station, photography. No experience required.

Sign up
Skills

Lend a skill

Photographers, designers, writers, audio/video people, event coordinators, fundraisers, social media — every one of these helps.

Tell us what you do
Stories

Share an oral history

Long-time residents, family stories, business histories — we'd love to record a short conversation for the centennial archive.

Share a story
Photos

Contribute photos

From shoeboxes, from yearbooks, from your phone — historic or current images of Sherman Oaks help fill out the public record.

Submit photos
Venues

Offer a venue

Storefront, school, library, hall, restaurant, courtyard. If you have a space the centennial could use for an event, we want to hear from you.

Offer a space
Sponsorship

Sponsor the year

Local businesses, civic groups, and family foundations can underwrite events, programming, and public art.

See sponsor tiers
Schools

Bring it into the classroom

We're partnering with local schools for centennial curriculum, art contests, and youth-led history projects.

School partnership
Civic Groups

Partner as an org

Neighborhood council, faith community, business district, club, league — there's a role for organized groups at the centennial.

Partner with us
Pitch

Pitch an event

Got an idea for a centennial event that doesn't quite fit anywhere else? Send it over. We'd rather hear from you than not.

Pitch an idea
