Centennial Sponsorship

Power a year of community.

Sherman Oaks 100 is a community-led celebration funded by local businesses, civic groups, and individual neighbors. Sponsorship makes everything on this site possible — free public events, public art, youth programs, and a year of community life.

Why Sponsor

Real community visibility — and real impact.

Centennial sponsors get year-long recognition tied to the most visible community celebration this neighborhood will host in our lifetimes. And every sponsorship dollar funds programming for residents, families, schools, and partner organizations.

Visibility

A full year of presence

Sponsors are recognized across the centennial year — at events, in print, online, and in physical signage at marquee gatherings.

Impact

Funds community programming

Every dollar funds free or low-cost public programs — block parties, school partnerships, public art, oral history.

Legacy

A name on a lasting moment

A hundred years happens once. Sponsors are recognized in the permanent centennial archive — the version that gets remembered.
