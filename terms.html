5. Stories, photographs and ideas you send us

The Site invites you to email us stories, photographs, map suggestions and other material (“submissions”). If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to help you send a submission; we will need their permission before we publish it.

  • You keep ownership. Sending a submission does not transfer your copyright to us.
  • We ask before publishing. We will not publish a story or photograph, or your name with it, until you give us permission. We will confirm in writing what we may do with it (for example, use on this Site, in a pop-up museum or in centennial materials) and how to credit it.
  • Reviewing your submission. By sending a submission, you allow us to store, copy and share it within the Committee so we can review it and reply to you.
  • Ideas and suggestions. If you send us an idea for an event, a map location or an improvement to the Site, we may use it without paying you or crediting you, although we try to credit people when we can.

When you send a submission, you confirm that:

  • you created it or have the right to share it, and you have told us who took a photograph if you know;
  • it is accurate to the best of your knowledge; and
  • you have permission from anyone who can be identified in it, or from a parent or guardian if that person is under 18, before it is published.

We may decline, edit for length or clarity (with your agreement for anything published under your name), or later remove any submission. You can ask us to remove your material from the Site at any time.
