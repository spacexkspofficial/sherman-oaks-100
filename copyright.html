Copyright Notice.

Who owns what on this website, what you are welcome to share, and how to tell us about a copyright concern.

Effective date: October 16, 2026
Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. Ownership

Except where a credit says otherwise, the text, design, graphics and centennial seal on shermanoaks100.com (the “Site”) are © 2026 Sherman Oaks 100 Centennial Committee (“the Committee,” “we”), or are used with the permission of their creators. The centennial logo was designed by Adam Khalid / Rock Solid Design.

“Sherman Oaks 100” and the centennial seal identify the centennial celebration. Please do not use them in a way that suggests the Committee sponsors or endorses something without our written permission.

2. Photographs and other third-party material

Photographs on the Site are credited beside each image. Historical photographs belong to the photographers, collections or other rights holders named in their credits, and recent photographs credited “Sherman Oaks 100” were taken for the centennial. Third-party material appears with permission or as otherwise allowed by law.

We cannot give permission to reuse material we do not own. To reuse a historical photograph, contact the rights holder named in its credit.

3. Names and trademarks

Businesses, buildings, products and organizations are named on the Site for historical and informational reference. Their names and marks belong to their owners. Naming them does not mean they sponsor, endorse or are affiliated with the centennial unless the Site says so.

4. What you are welcome to do

  • View and print pages for your personal, non-commercial use.
  • Share links to any page of the Site, anywhere.
  • Quote short passages of our text, with the credit “Sherman Oaks 100” and a link to the page you are quoting.
  • Share centennial event information to promote taking part in the events.

5. Uses that need permission

Please ask us first before you:

  • reproduce substantial parts of the Site’s text in print or online;
  • use the centennial seal, name or logo on merchandise, packaging, signage or anywhere it could suggest an official connection; or
  • use any Site content or branding commercially.

To ask, email info@shermanoaks100.com with the subject “Permission request” and a short description of what you would like to use, and how and where. For photographs, see section 2. When we give permission, please credit “Sherman Oaks 100 Centennial Committee” and link to the page where practical.

6. Report a copyright concern

If you believe material on the Site uses your work without permission or without the right credit, email info@shermanoaks100.com with the subject “Copyright concern.” Please include:

  • the work you believe is affected, and where it appears on the Site (the page address and a description of the item);
  • your name, email address and, if you like, a phone number;
  • a statement that you are the rights holder or are authorized to act for the rights holder; and
  • a statement that, in good faith, you believe the use is not authorized and that the information in your message is accurate.

We will reply promptly. While we look into a concern about a photograph or other third-party material, we will normally remove it from the Site until the question is resolved. If we have credited your work incorrectly, we will fix the credit.
